# ICC World Test Championship (WTC) Records

Data transcribed from ESPNcricinfo Records pages (screenshots provided by user),
as displayed on 12 Sep 2026. Lists are partial (top ~15-30 entries per category,
as visible in the screenshots), not the complete all-time lists.

## Files
- most_runs.csv — Batting: Most runs (career)
- most_wickets.csv — Bowling: Most wickets (career)
- best_bowling_innings.csv — Bowling: Best figures in an innings
- best_bowling_match.csv — Bowling: Best figures in a match
- most_5wi_career.csv — Bowling: Most five-wicket hauls in an innings (career)
- most_10wm_career.csv — Bowling: Most ten-wicket hauls in a match (career)
- best_economy_rate_career.csv — Bowling: Best career economy rates
- best_economy_rate_innings.csv — Bowling: Best economy rates in an innings
- most_runs_conceded_innings.csv — Bowling: Most runs conceded in an innings

Note: some rows/columns may be incomplete where screenshots were cut off or
partially obscured by ads/UI elements.
